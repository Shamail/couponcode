# OPS-04: Buyer Onboarding

## 1. Executive Summary

This document defines the buyer acquisition and onboarding playbook for Frictionless. The focus is rapid activation in dense corridors through a mix of field marketing, referrals, and in-app education.

Success is measured by activation rate (install → first deal view) and early retention.

## 2. Acquisition Channels

| Channel | Tactic | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street Teams | QR flyers near corridors | Best for first-city launch |
| Merchant Cross-Promo | Posters + in-store QR | Low CAC, high intent |
| Referral | “Invite a friend, earn points” | Incentivize share |
| Social | Local micro-influencers | Tie to corridor events |

## 3. Activation Funnel

1. Install
2. OTP signup
3. Location permission
4. First map view
5. First deal claim

## 4. Onboarding Messaging

- “Deals only appear when you are nearby.”
- “Keep location on for better accuracy.”
- “Claim quickly — deals expire fast.”

## 5. Field Activation Checklist

- [ ] Deploy street team to key corridors
- [ ] Set up merchant posters with QR
- [ ] Run a “Savings Weekend” activation
- [ ] Track daily activation rate
