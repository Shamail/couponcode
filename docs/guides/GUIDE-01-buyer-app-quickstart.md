# GUIDE-01: Buyer App Quickstart

## 1. Executive Summary

This guide helps buyers get started with the Frictionless app in under five minutes. It covers installation, permissions, deal discovery, and redemption.

Frictionless is built around real-time location. The more accurate the location, the better the deals you will see.

## 2. Install & Sign In

1. Download **Frictionless** from the App Store or Google Play
2. Open the app and enter your phone number
3. Verify your OTP code

## 3. Enable Location (Required)

Location access powers nearby deals and deal distance accuracy.

- **iOS:** Select **Allow While Using App**
- **Android:** Select **Allow only while using the app**

## 4. Discover Deals

1. Open the map and look for **deal** markers
2. Tap a deal marker to see the deal card
3. Review the deal details, distance, and time remaining

## 5. Claim a Deal

1. Tap **Claim Deal**
2. The app shows your **SafeColor Verification** QR
3. Keep your screen bright for the scan

## 6. Redeem in Store

1. Show the QR to the cashier
2. Cashier scans with the Seller app
3. Both screens confirm with matching colors if successful

## 7. Tips for Better Results

- Keep your GPS on while walking in shopping districts
- Start with a smaller radius (200-300m) for dense corridors
- Check the app on weekends for more active deals

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| No deals visible | Move to a denser street or increase radius |
| QR not scanning | Increase brightness and hold steady |
| Location feels off | Toggle GPS or restart the app |
