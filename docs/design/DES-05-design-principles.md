# DES-05: Design Principles

## Executive Summary

These principles guide every design decision in Frictionless. They prioritize clarity, speed, and trust in a map-first, savings-focused product.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Clarity over clutter**
   - Value is visible in one glance.
2. **Map-first utility**
   - Location is core, not decorative.
3. **Calm confidence**
   - Motion and color are subtle, not loud.
4. **Low friction actions**
   - Claim and redeem in as few steps as possible.
5. **Trust by default**
   - Transparent terms, consistent UI, no surprises.
6. **Local by design**
   - Language, content, and visuals reflect Morocco.

---

## 2. Decision Framework

Ask before shipping:
- Does the user understand the value in under 3 seconds?
- Are we hiding critical info behind extra taps?
- Does the flow reduce steps or add them?
- Is the UI stable for low connectivity?
- Is the experience equally clear in Arabic and French?

---

## 3. Anti-Patterns

- Over-animated UI that feels game-like
- Neon colors or heavy gradients on core screens
- Crowded cards with competing actions
- Large empty states that hide next steps

---

## 4. Success Indicators

- Short time-to-claim
- High repeat redemption
- Low cancellation or dispute rates
- Fast comprehension in usability tests
