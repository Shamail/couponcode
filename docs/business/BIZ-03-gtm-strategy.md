# BIZ-03: Go-to-Market Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the GTM strategy for Frictionless, focusing on corridor-based launches, merchant-first acquisition, and rapid buyer activation. The plan is designed to create local density before expanding to new cities.

The strategy blends field sales, merchant incentives, and community-driven buyer activation.

## 2. Launch Phases

### 2.1 Phase 1: Corridor Domination (Months 1-3)
- Target: 50 merchants, 1,000 buyers
- Focus: Maarif + Gauthier
- Goal: 100 redemptions in month 1

### 2.2 Phase 2: City Expansion (Months 4-6)
- Target: 200 merchants, 5,000 buyers
- Expand to Ain Diab + downtown
- Launch Pro tier for high performers

### 2.3 Phase 3: Multi-City Scale (Months 7-12)
- Add Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier
- Deploy local merchant teams
- Build regional partnerships

## 3. Merchant Acquisition Motion

| Step | Tactic | KPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify | Walk corridors + build lead list | Leads/week |
| Demo | On-site Seller app demo | Demo → signup |
| Activate | First deal within 7 days | Activation rate |
| Retain | Monthly ROI review | Merchant churn |

## 4. Buyer Acquisition Motion

- **Street Teams**: QR flyers + live demo near corridors
- **Referral Program**: “Invite a friend, earn points”
- **Merchant Amplification**: In-store signage + receipts
- **Micro-Influencers**: Neighborhood content creators

## 5. Partnerships

- Retail associations and mall operators
- Transit corridors (tramway stations)
- Local events and pop-up markets
