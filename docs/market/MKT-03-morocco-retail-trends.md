# MKT-03: Morocco Retail Trends

## 1. Executive Summary

Morocco's urbanization and digital adoption create favorable conditions for a location-first retail discovery platform. High mobile connectivity and dense urban corridors support rapid, block-by-block marketplace activation.

This document highlights macro trends that shape Frictionless' opportunity: growing urban concentration, rising smartphone and internet penetration, and increased dependence on mobile channels for discovery and commerce.

## 2. Macro Trends

### 2.1 Urbanization and City Density
- Urban population has grown rapidly, reaching roughly two-thirds of the population in 2024 and projected to continue rising through 2030. (See APPENDIX-B: S-02)
- Economic activity and retail consumption are concentrated in large coastal cities and dense corridors. (See APPENDIX-B: S-02)

### 2.2 Digital Adoption
- Mobile connections significantly exceed population due to multi-SIM behavior, creating ubiquitous device presence. (See APPENDIX-B: S-01)
- Internet penetration is above 90%, indicating a large addressable digital audience in urban areas. (See APPENDIX-B: S-01)
- Social platforms influence discovery, but do not translate reliably into foot traffic conversion. (See APPENDIX-B: S-01)

### 2.3 Payments and On-Device Behavior
- Digital wallet and card adoption are growing, but cash remains prevalent. (See APPENDIX-B: S-06, S-07)
- QR redemption is compatible with both cash and digital payments, reducing integration friction.

## 3. Seasonal Retail Rhythms

| Season | Expected Behavior | Opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ramadan | Increased evening foot traffic | Time-shifted flash deals |
| Summer (July-Aug) | Tourism + local travel | Geo-targeted tourist offers |
| Back-to-school | Category spikes in apparel + supplies | Partner bundles |
| Winter sales | Discount-driven behavior | Competitive deal wars |

## 4. Corridor Prioritization (Hypothesis)

| City | High-Density Corridors | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Casablanca | Maarif, Gauthier, Ain Diab | High retail density + transit |
| Rabat | Agdal, Hassan | Walkable commercial zones |
| Marrakech | Gueliz, Hivernage | Tourism + lifestyle retail |
| Tangier | City Center, Malabata | Mixed local + tourist demand |

## 5. Implications for Frictionless

1. **Launch in dense, walkable corridors** where discovery and conversion are fastest.
2. **Optimize for mobile-first behavior** with low-latency map interactions.
3. **Align promotions to temporal rhythms** like Ramadan evenings and weekend peaks.
4. **Support cash-first workflows** with QR validation that does not require payment integration.
