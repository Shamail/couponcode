# BRAND-07: Imagery & Photography

## Executive Summary

Imagery should feel local, warm, and real. Use photos that reflect everyday Moroccan life—cafés, markets, and neighborhood storefronts—without exaggeration or staged glam.

---

## 1. Visual Style

| Attribute | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Authentic | Real locations, natural people |
| Clean | Minimal clutter, focused subject |
| Warm | Soft daylight, golden tones |
| Practical | Emphasize savings moments |

---

## 2. Subject Matter

**Preferred**
- Neighborhood cafés, bakeries, and small retail
- Friendly interactions between people and merchants
- Products being purchased or redeemed
- Local signage and recognizable city textures

**Avoid**
- Overly staged studio shots
- Heavy luxury or flashy scenes
- Stock images that feel generic or non-local

---

## 3. Composition & Cropping

- Use **rule of thirds** with space for overlay text.
- Maintain a **safe text zone** of 20% from edges.
- Prefer **portrait or 4:5** crops for mobile marketing.

---

## 4. Color & Lighting

- Natural daylight or warm indoor lighting
- Avoid harsh neon or saturated color casts
- Keep shadows soft and neutral

---

## 5. Overlay & Text Treatment

- Use semi-opaque overlays (`#121212` at 40-60%) to ensure text legibility.
- Keep text minimal (1-2 lines).
- Align typography to BRAND-05 scale.

---

## 6. Cultural Considerations

- Represent a diverse range of Moroccan backgrounds and regions.
- Avoid sensitive or private spaces without consent.
- Ensure attire and settings reflect local norms.

---

## 7. Asset Sources

- Prefer original photography.
- If stock is needed, use vetted sources and log license data in BRAND-11.
