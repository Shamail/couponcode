# BRAND-10: Brand Applications

## Executive Summary

This document defines how the Frictionless brand should appear across product surfaces, marketing, and merchant-facing materials. Consistency across touchpoints builds trust and recognition.

---

## 1. App Icon & Splash

- Use the approved icon from BRAND-03.
- Splash screens should be minimal: logo centered on `background.primary`.
- Avoid taglines on splash screens.

---

## 2. App Store Listings

| Element | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Title | “Frictionless: Nearby Deals” |
| Subtitle | “Discover savings around you” |
| Screenshots | 5-7, show map + wallet + redeem |
| Copy tone | Short, clear, no hype |

Use local imagery from BRAND-07 and maintain consistent typography.

---

## 3. Social & Marketing

- Use high-contrast overlays for legibility.
- Keep copy to **1 headline + 1 supporting line**.
- Use brand primary for buttons or highlights.

---

## 4. Merchant Materials

### In-store Signage
- QR poster must include merchant name and validity note
- Place QR in lower third with 20% safe area
- Use `brand.primary` for the call-to-action

### Table Tents
- Two-sided: “Save Here” / “Show this code to staff”

---

## 5. Co-Branding

- Equal prominence for partner and Frictionless
- Use approved lockups from BRAND-03
- Keep spacing at least 1x logo height

---

## 6. Email & Push Templates

- Subject lines: under 40 characters
- Push notifications: under 60 characters
- Always include distance + value (e.g., “25% off · 200m away”)
